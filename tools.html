No-Vig Fair Odds Calculator
Enter both sides of a market to strip the bookmaker's vig and find the true implied probabilities.
How it works: Books price both sides with a margin baked in — e.g., -110/-110 = 52.4% each side, which sums to 104.8% (the 4.8% is the vig). This calculator removes that margin to show the true implied probabilities.
Kelly Criterion Stake Calculator
Calculates mathematically optimal stake size to maximize long-run bankroll growth given your edge.
Formula: Kelly % = (p × b − q) / b, where p = win probability, q = 1−p, b = decimal odds − 1.
Tip: Half Kelly is strongly recommended for prop betting — it reduces variance while retaining ~75% of expected growth.
Expected Value Calculator
Calculate the expected value of a bet given your edge over the market.
Formula: EV = (prob × profit) − ((1 − prob) × stake). A positive EV means the bet is mathematically profitable over a large sample.

Parlay Calculator
Build a parlay with up to 10 legs. Enter American odds for each leg.
Correlated parlays: Legs within the same game (e.g. NRFI + low-scoring team) can be correlated — meaning if one hits, the other is more likely to hit. Standard parlay calculators assume independence; correlated plays can have higher true EV than shown.
Closing Line Value (CLV) Converter
Enter the price you took and the closing price. CLV measures whether you beat the market — the most reliable indicator of long-run edge.
How to read CLV:
Positive CLV = you got a better price than closing → you were on the right side of the market.
Negative CLV = market moved against you → steam moved the other way after you bet.
Why it matters: Consistent positive CLV (closing at worse odds than you took) is the #1 predictor of long-run profitability — more reliable than win rate over small samples. Long-run, every +1% CLV edge translates to roughly +1% ROI on flat stakes.
Same-Game Parlay (SGP) Builder
Select a game, pick prop legs, and see correlation-adjusted fair odds vs. what books typically charge for SGPs.
Loading player props…
Select 2–5 legs to build your SGP. Correlations are estimated from same-team / market relationships.
Correlation adjustment: Same-game prop legs aren't independent — a batter hitting increases their RBI chance (positive correlation). Books price SGPs with a 10-15% vig penalty on top of standard vig to account for this. This tool shows you the correlation-adjusted fair price and compares it to a typical book's SGP offer.
Formula: P(A∩B) ≈ P(A)·P(B) + r·√(P(A)·Q(A)·P(B)·Q(B)) where r is the estimated pairwise correlation.
